# Project Plan: Kaure Ocean Educational & Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Executive Summary
Kaure Ocean Educational & Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. (Kaure Ocean Japanese Language School) is a licensed overseas employment and educational consultancy based in Kathmandu, Nepal (License No: 221/082/083, Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security, Department of Foreign Employment). Led by CEO Prakash Bastola, the company specializes in Japanese language education, technical skill development, cultural orientation, workplace safety training, and facilitation of SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) and TITP (Technical Intern Training Program) pathways to Japan and global markets.

This project delivers a production-ready, enterprise-grade web application and management system incorporating a public marketing portal, student portal, and comprehensive admin CMS.

## 2. Objectives & Scope
- **Public Website:** Responsive, high-performance web presence mirroring top educational consultancy standards (benchmarked against reference design https://krishna.edu.np/).
- **Service Portals:** Dedicated sections for Japanese Language School, SSW/TITP Programs, Train-Deploy-Support approach, and Training Methodology.
- **Enquiry & Consultation System:** Multi-step intake forms, appointment scheduling, and WhatsApp/LINE direct integration.
- **Management Dashboard:** Secure admin panel for content management, candidate application tracking, enquiry moderation, and draft statistical verification.

## 3. Phased Development Plan
- **Phase 00:** Project Initialization & Reference Analysis (Current)
- **Phase 01:** Database Migration & Models Setup
- **Phase 02:** Public Website Frontend & Blade Layouts (Bootstrap 5, Custom CSS, Branding)
- **Phase 03:** Dynamic Features (Enquiry Forms, Course Catalog, SSW/TITP Programs, Testimonials)
- **Phase 04:** Admin Management Panel & CMS
- **Phase 05:** Testing, Optimization & Production Readiness

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- Full fidelity to Kaure Ocean branding (#203070 Navy, #E05020 Orange) and brochure data.
- Zero leftover template references (e.g., Borcelle).
- Verified draft handling for conflicting brochure statistics.
- Clean Laravel 11/13 architecture with passing tests and lint checks.
