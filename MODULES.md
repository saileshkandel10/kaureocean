# Modules Specification: Kaure Ocean Platform

## 1. Public Website Module (`/`)
- Homepage with Hero, Train-Deploy-Support workflow, stats banner, featured programs, partners slider, and testimonials.
- About Us page including CEO Message from Prakash Bastola and official license details (221/082/083).
- Japanese Language School module detailing curriculum, levels (N5-N1), and teaching faculty.
- Programs & Services module covering SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) and TITP (Technical Intern Training Program).
- Training & Support module covering technical skill development, cultural orientation, and workplace safety training.
- Contact page with interactive map, operating hours, direct phone/email, and enquiry submission.

## 2. Candidate Engagement Module
- Consultation booking and appointment scheduling.
- Online Enquiry submission with instant notification triggers.
- Resource center / brochure download and informational guides.

## 3. Administrative CMS Module (`/admin`)
- Secure Dashboard with key metrics (Total Enquiries, Active Programs, Verified Testimonials).
- Program & Course management (CRUD operations).
- Enquiry management & status tracking.
- Testimonial & Partner moderation.
- Draft Statistics verification panel (managing brochure stats).
- CMS content editor for static page copy.
